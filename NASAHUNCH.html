NASA HUNCH Project

LunarLink Locker

Our team is participating in the 2026–2027 NASA HUNCH program. Our project is called LunarLink Locker. It focuses on creating a multipurpose storage system that could be useful for astronauts on the Moon.

Our goal is to create a design that can be assembled in different ways to serve multiple purposes while making good use of available space.

Project Design

This image shows our LunarLink Locker project design.

The Challenge

Astronauts need storage systems that are organized, useful, and able to fit into limited spaces. Our challenge is to design a locker that can be reused and rearranged for different purposes.

Our Solution

Our design uses stackable panels with pins, clips, latches, and a handle. The panels can be connected in different arrangements.

The locker can be redesigned into structures such as a wall, arch, road, table, or bench. This makes the design more flexible and multipurpose.
