NASA HUNCH

Multi-Purpose Lunar Locker

For NASA HUNCH 2026-2027, my team is developing a Multi-Purpose Lunar Locker. Our goal is to design a storage system that can be used for different purposes in a lunar environment.

Our Design

Our design uses stackable panels that can be connected and rearranged into different structures. Some possible uses include a table, bench, wall, or other useful structures.

The design focuses on making the locker useful for different needs.
